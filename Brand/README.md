# Zoop identity

Refined Fold is the approved Zoop mark. Two separated angular strokes form a Z,
with angled terminals and a tight central gap.

- `zoop-mark.svg`: transparent, editable vector mark; uses `currentColor`.
- `zoop-icon.svg`: square icon source with the app background and light mark.
- `../App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon-1024.png`: opaque 1024 px iOS export.

Keep the central gap, proportions and flat colors. Let iOS apply the icon mask;
do not bake rounded corners into the source. In the app icon, scale the mark to 84.6%
around the center for breathing room. Export the icon without an alpha channel.
